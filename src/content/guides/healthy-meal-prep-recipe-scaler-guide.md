## 1. Recipe ko Easily Scale (Double / Half / 10x) Kaise Karein?

Aksar cookbook ya YouTube recipes 2 ya 4 servings ke hisaab se di hoti hain. Jab aap pure hafte ke 8 meals bana rahe hon ya 12 logon ki dinner party host kar rahe hon, toh ingredient calculation mein gadbad ho jati hai.

### Universal Scaling Factor Formula:

$$\text{Scaling Factor (SF)} = \frac{\text{Desired Number of Servings}}{\text{Original Recipe Servings}}$$

$$\text{New Ingredient Quantity} = \text{Original Quantity} \times \text{Scaling Factor}$$

#### Example: High-Protein Paneer Bhurji
- **Original Recipe (Serves 2):**
  - Paneer: 200g
  - Onion: 1 medium (100g)
  - Tomatoes: 2 medium (150g)
  - Olive Oil: 10 ml
  - Jeera (Cumin): 1 tsp (5g)

Agar aap **5 din ka lunch prep (10 servings)** bana rahe hain:
$$\text{Scaling Factor} = \frac{10}{2} = 5.0$$

- Paneer: $200\text{g} \times 5 = \mathbf{1,000\text{g (1 kg)}}$
- Onion: $100\text{g} \times 5 = \mathbf{500\text{g}}$
- Tomatoes: $150\text{g} \times 5 = \mathbf{750\text{g}}$
- Olive Oil: $10\text{ ml} \times 5 = \mathbf{50\text{ ml}}$

### The Golden "Spices & Leavening" Exception:
- **Baking Powder, Yeast & Salt:** Agar recipe 4x se zyada scale ho rahi hai, toh leavening agents aur salt ko 0.75× scaling factor se badhayein taaki dish khati ya over-salted na ho.
- **Teekha Masala & Chili:** Spices ko gradually add karein aur taste test karte rahein.

## 2. Macronutrient Ratio Kya Hai? (Protein, Carbs & Fats Breakdown)

Hamari daily calories teen primary macronutrients se aati hain:
1. **Protein:** 1 gram = **4 Calories** (Muscle repair, enzymatic functions, satiety)
2. **Carbohydrates:** 1 gram = **4 Calories** (Immediate glycogen energy, brain fuel)
3. **Fats:** 1 gram = **9 Calories** (Hormone production, vitamin absorption, cell membrane)

### Goal-Based Macro Split Blueprint:

| Goal | Protein % | Carbs % | Fats % | Kiske Liye Best Hai? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fat Loss / Cutting** | 40% | 35% | 25% | High satiety, muscle preservation while in calorie deficit |
| **Lean Muscle Building** | 30% | 50% | 20% | High glycogen for heavy strength training & progressive overload |
| **Endurance Athlete** | 20% | 60% | 20% | Marathon runners, cyclists, high-stamina sports |
| **Keto / Low-Carb** | 25% | 5% | 70% | Ketosis metabolic adaptation |
| **General Health & Longevity** | 25% | 50% | 25% | Balanced everyday lifestyle |

## 3. Practical Meal Prep Workflow: Sunday Batch Cooking Blueprint

Hafte mein do baar (Sunday aur Wednesday) 90 minute ka batch cooking session aapke pooray hafte ke 15 ghante bacha sakta hai:

1. **Step 1: Carb Base Prepare Karein (25 Mins)**
   - Ek saath 400g Brown Rice, Quinoa ya Sweet Potatoes boil karke glass containers mein portion kar lein.
2. **Step 2: Protein Cooking (35 Mins)**
   - Marinated Paneer / Tofu / Chicken breast / Boiled Chickpeas (Chana) ko air-fry ya roast karein.
3. **Step 3: Fibrous Vegetables (20 Mins)**
   - Broccoli, bell peppers, carrots aur beans ko halka saute ya steam karein (crunchy texture retain karein).
4. **Step 4: Sauces & Dressings Alag Rakhein**
   - Salad dressings ya curries ko dry vegetables ke sath mix karke na rakhein, warna veggies soggy ho jaati hain. Khane se 2 minute pehle add karein.

## 4. Healthy Khana Banane ke 5 Practical Kitchen Hacks

- **Use an Oil Spray Bottle:** Ek chamach tel mein 120 calories hoti hain. Direct bottle se tel daalne par 30-40 ml tel chala jata hai. Oil spray use karne se per dish sirf 3-5 ml tel lagta hai, saving 300+ calories daily!
- **Digital Kitchen Food Scale:** Andaza hamesha galat hota hai. 100g cooked dal vs 100g raw dal mein 3x calories ka fark hota hai. Food ko raw state mein weigh karein.
- **Glass Airtight Containers (Pyrex/Borosil):** Plastic containers mein microplastics aur food smell absorb hoti hai. Glass containers microwave-safe hote hain aur freshness 4 din tak lock rehti hai.
- **Calorie per Serving Calculation:** Pure pot ki total calories ko total containers se divide karein using our [Calorie per Serving Tool](/food/calorie-per-serving-calculator/).
