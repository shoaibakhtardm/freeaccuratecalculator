## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How does a BTC SIP calculator use Dollar-Cost Averaging to lower risk?
A BTC SIP calculator models automated, periodic investments of a fixed currency amount across regular intervals. 

By keeping your contribution constant, you automatically acquire more satoshis when Bitcoin prices drop and fewer when prices rise. Over a multi-year cycle, this systematic process significantly lowers your average purchase price and shields you from emotional market timing errors.

### 2. Why do exchange-specific tools like a CoinDCX SIP calculator matter?
Commercial crypto exchanges charge distinct fees, including maker/taker trading fees, order execution minimums, and in India, a statutory 1% Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194S. 

An exchange-aligned calculator accounts for these operational frictions, helping you determine whether a weekly or monthly frequency best maximizes your net coin accumulation.

### 3. How are crypto SIP profits taxed under Indian law?
Under Section 115BBH of the Indian Income Tax Act, net gains from the transfer of Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) are taxed at a flat **30% (plus a 4% cess, totaling 31.2%)**. 

No deduction is permitted other than the direct cost of acquisition, and losses from one cryptocurrency token cannot be used to offset profits from another. Every sale also triggers an immediate 1% TDS deduction under Section 194S.
