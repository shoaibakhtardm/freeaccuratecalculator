## 2. Rental Yield Kya Hai aur Ise Kaise Calculate Karein?

**Rental Yield** wo percentage return hai jo aapko property se aane wale annual rent ke roop mein milta hai.

### A. Gross Rental Yield Formula
Gross yield sabse aasan calculation hai, jisme koi kharcha minus nahi hota:

$$\text{Gross Rental Yield (\%)} = \frac{\text{Annual Gross Rental Income}}{\text{Total Property Cost}} \times 100$$

#### Example:
- Property Purchase Price + Registration: ₹60,00,000
- Monthly Rent: ₹18,000 (Annual: ₹18,000 × 12 = ₹2,16,000)
$$\text{Gross Yield} = \frac{2,16,000}{60,00,000} \times 100 = \mathbf{3.6\%}$$

### B. Net Rental Yield Formula (The Real Picture)
Real life mein property ke sath kaafi kharche jude hote hain: maintenance, municipal property tax, broker commission, tenant vacancy periods, insurance, aur society sinking fund.

$$\text{Net Rental Yield (\%)} = \frac{\text{Annual Rent} - \text{Annual Operating Expenses}}{\text{Total Property Acquisition Cost}} \times 100$$

Agar upar diye gaye example mein:
- Annual Rent: ₹2,16,000
- Annual Expenses (Maintenance ₹30,000 + Property Tax ₹12,000 + 1 Month Vacancy Loss ₹18,000): ₹60,000
- Net Annual Income: ₹1,56,000
$$\text{Net Yield} = \frac{1,56,000}{60,00,000} \times 100 = \mathbf{2.6\%}$$

Ye 2.6% aapka actual pocket return hai!

## 3. Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate) Kya Hai?

**Cap Rate** commercial aur multi-family real estate valuation ka global standard hai. Ye batata hai ki bina kisi debt ya mortgage loan ke, ek property market value ke comparison mein kitna operational cash flow generate kar rahi hai.

$$\text{Cap Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Net Operating Income (NOI)}}{\text{Current Market Value of Property}} \times 100$$

### NOI (Net Operating Income) Kaise Nikala Jata Hai?
$$\text{NOI} = \text{Effective Gross Income} - \text{Operating Expenses}$$
*(Note: NOI mein mortgage principal aur interest repayment shamil nahi hota; ye pure property performance ko evaluate karta hai.)*

### Cap Rate vs Rental Yield: Kya Farak Hai?
* **Rental Yield:** Investor ke total investment cost par aane wala return dekhta hai (residential buyers ke liye primary).
* **Cap Rate:** Property ki current market price par pure unleveraged profitability evaluate karta hai (institutional aur commercial investors ke liye benchmark).

## 4. In Dono Metrics ko Compare Karke Sahi Property Kaise Chunein?

| Property Type | Typical Gross Yield | Typical Cap Rate | Risk Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Residential (Tier-1 City Apartment)** | 2.5% – 3.5% | 2.0% – 3.0% | Low vacancy risk, high liquidity, steady appreciation |
| **Co-Living / Student Housing** | 5.5% – 7.5% | 5.0% – 6.5% | Moderate operational hassle, high tenant turnover |
| **Commercial Office Space / Retail** | 6.5% – 9.0% | 6.0% – 8.0% | High entry ticket, long lease lock-in, vacancy sensitivity |
| **Industrial / Warehousing** | 7.0% – 10.0% | 7.0% – 9.5% | High lease stability, specialized market demand |

### Golden Decision Matrix:
- Agar residential property ka **Net Yield < 2.5%** hai aur us area mein capital growth bhi stagnant hai, toh aisi property khareedna financial blunder ho sakta hai.
- Agar commercial unit ka **Cap Rate > 7.5%** hai aur tenant ek reputed MNC ya bank hai jiska 9-year lease lock-in hai, toh ye ek superb cash-flow asset hai.

## 5. Beginners ke Liye Real Estate Investment ke 5 Golden Rules

1. **The 1% Rule Reality Check:** Western countries mein 1% monthly rule mashhoor hai (Monthly Rent = 1% of Purchase Price), lekin Indian residential market mein ye lagbhag 0.25% - 0.35% hota hai. Isliye cash flow ke sath location appreciation par gehra focus rakhein.
2. **Vacancy Buffer Factor:** Kabhi bhi 100% occupancy assume na karein. Har saal kam se kam 1 se 1.5 mahine ka rent vacancy buffer mein alag rakhein.
3. **Tenant Quality > Maximum Rent:** 2,000 rupaye zyada rent ke lalach mein bina verification ya credit track record ke tenant na rakhein. Timely rent aur property care zyada kimti hain.
4. **Maintenance Reserves:** Purani buildings mein plumbing, seepage aur painting ke kharche rental yield ko khatam kar dete hain. Har mahine rent ka 10% sinking fund mein save karein.
5. **Tax on Rental Income:** Yaad rakhein ki rental income par Standard 30% deduction (Section 24a) aur municipal taxes minus hote hain. Baki amount par aapke tax slab ke hisaab se income tax lagta hai.
