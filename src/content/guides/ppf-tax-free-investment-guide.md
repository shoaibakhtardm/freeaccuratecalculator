The Public Provident Fund (PPF) remains one of India's most beloved risk-free, sovereign-guaranteed investment avenues. Established by the Government of India in 1968, PPF combines capital safety with compounding tax-free returns.

In this guide, we break down **how to calculate PPF returns**, explain the lucrative "Exempt-Exempt-Exempt" (EEE) status, and share the critical deposit-timing rule that boosts your interest every single year.

## 1. Key Features of the Public Provident Fund (PPF)

* **Sovereign Guarantee:** Backed 100% by the Central Government of India, making default risk mathematically zero.
* **Maturity Tenure:** 15 Financial Years (exclusive of the year of opening).
* **Deposit Limits:** Minimum ₹500 per year; Maximum ₹1,50,000 per financial year.
* **Interest Accrual:** Compounded annually and credited on March 31st of each financial year.
* **Loan and Partial Withdrawal:** Partial withdrawals permitted from Year 7; loans available from Year 3 to 6.

---

## 2. The Power of EEE Tax Status

Very few investment vehicles in India qualify for the triple-tax-exempt **EEE status**:
1. **Exempt on Investment:** Contributions up to ₹1,50,000 qualify for deduction under Section 80C (Old Tax Regime).
2. **Exempt on Accumulation:** The annual interest credited to your account is completely exempt from income tax.
3. **Exempt on Maturity:** The entire lump-sum maturity corpus is withdrawn with **zero capital gains tax or TDS**.

*Comparison:* While Fixed Deposits (FD) offer similar interest rates, the interest is fully taxable at your income tax slab (up to 30%+), significantly depressing your real post-tax yield.

## 4. The Critical "5th of the Month" Rule

The PPF rules explicitly state that interest is computed on the **lowest balance between the close of the 5th day and the end of the month**.

* **Scenario A:** You deposit ₹1,50,000 on **April 4th**. You earn interest on that full amount for all 12 months of the financial year.
* **Scenario B:** You deposit ₹1,50,000 on **April 6th**. You lose an entire month of interest on ₹1.5 Lakh—costing you ₹887.50 in Year 1 alone, which compounds into tens of thousands lost over 15 years!

*Rule of Thumb:* Always execute your PPF transfer between **April 1st and April 5th** for maximum annual interest.
