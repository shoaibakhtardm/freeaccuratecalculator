## 1. Car Depreciation Kya Hai aur Ye Value Kaise Ghatati Hai?

**Vehicle Depreciation** ka matlab hai car ki resale value ka samay ke sath kam hona. Car koi real estate ya mutual fund nahi hai jo appreciate ho; ye ek mechanical asset hai jo use hone ke sath-sath ghisata hai aur purana hota hai.

### Standard Car Depreciation Curve:
* **Day 1 (Showroom se bahar nikalte hi):** 8% – 10% instant loss (Registration aur ex-showroom gap).
* **Year 1:** 15% – 20% depreciation.
* **Year 3:** 35% – 45% depreciation.
* **Year 5:** 50% – 60% depreciation.

### Depreciation Calculation Formula:
Standard reducing-balance depreciation method:
$$\text{Depreciated Value} = \text{Purchase Price} \times (1 - d)^t$$
Jahan $d$ annual depreciation rate hai (e.g., 0.15) aur $t$ saalon ki sankhya hai.

#### Example: ₹12 Lakh ki Car ka 5-Year Reality Check
- Khareedte waqt: ₹12,00,000
- 5 saal baad market resale value: ~₹5,40,000
- **Total Loss in Depreciation: ₹6,60,000** (Lagbhag ₹1,10,000 har saal gayab!)

## 2. Fuel Cost Calculation: Daily Commute ka Real Impact

Fuel kharcha car ki life mein doosra sabse bada cash outflow hai.

$$\text{Monthly Fuel Cost} = \left( \frac{\text{Monthly Running Distance (km)}}{\text{Vehicle Fuel Mileage (km/L)}} \right) \times \text{Fuel Price per Liter}$$

### Comparison: Daily 40 km Commute (Monthly 1,200 km)
Maante hain Petrol = ₹100/L, Diesel = ₹90/L, EV Electricity = ₹8/unit (consumption 0.15 kWh/km).

| Parameter | Petrol Car (14 km/L) | Diesel Car (18 km/L) | Electric Car (EV) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Fuel Liters/Units** | 85.7 Liters | 66.7 Liters | 180 kWh |
| **Monthly Fuel Expense** | **₹8,571** | **₹6,000** | **₹1,440** |
| **Annual Fuel Expense** | **₹1,02,852** | **₹72,000** | **₹17,280** |
| **5-Year Fuel Expense** | **₹5,14,260** | **₹3,60,000** | **₹86,400** |

Sirf fuel mein ek EV 5 saal mein **₹4.2 Lakh se zyada** bacha sakti hai!

## 3. Nayi vs Purani Car: 5-Saal ka Comprehensive Cost Analysis

Aaiye compare karte hain: Ek **Nayi Car (₹12 Lakh)** khareedna vs ek **3-Saal Purani Certified Used Car (₹6.5 Lakh)** khareedna:

## 4. Auto Loan Interest aur EMI ko Budget mein Shamil Karna

Car loan lete waqt "20/4/10 Rule" ko yaad rakhein:
- **20% Down Payment:** Kam se kam 20% on-road price cash down payment karein.
- **4 Years Maximum Tenure:** Loan tenure 4 saal se zyada na rakhein, warna gaadi ki market value uske loan balance se kam ho jayegi (Negative Equity trap).
- **10% of Income:** Total car expenses (EMI + Fuel + Insurance) aapki monthly in-hand salary ke 10% se 15% se zyada nahi hone chahiye.

## 5. Smart Car Buying Checklist

1. **On-Road vs Ex-Showroom:** Ex-showroom price mein RTO tax, insurance, fastag aur handling charges jodkar on-road price 15-18% badh jaati hai.
2. **Insurance Self-Quote:** Showroom dealers insurance par mota commission lete hain. Bahar se policy compare karke aap ₹20,000 se ₹35,000 bacha sakte hain.
3. **Resale Value Brands:** Toyota, Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai jaise brands ka depreciation slow hota hai jabki niche luxury brands ka depreciation bohot brutal hota hai.
4. **Maintenance Schedule:** European cars mein parts aur labor costs Asian cars ke comparison mein 2x se 3x zyada hoti hain.
