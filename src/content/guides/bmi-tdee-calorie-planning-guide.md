## 1. BMI aur TDEE mein Kya Antar Hai?

Bahut se log BMI, BMR aur TDEE ke beech confuse ho jaate hain. In teeno ka role alag hai:

### 1. BMI (Body Mass Index)
BMI aapki **height aur weight ka ratio** batata hai. Ye batata hai ki aapki lambai ke hisaab se aapka vajan normal hai, kam hai (underweight), ya zyada hai (overweight/obese).

$$\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{Weight in kg}}{(\text{Height in meters})^2}$$

#### WHO (World Health Organization) BMI Ranges:
* **Underweight:** BMI < 18.5
* **Normal / Healthy Weight:** BMI 18.5 – 24.9 *(Asian adults ke liye ideal: 18.5 – 22.9)*
* **Overweight:** BMI 25.0 – 29.9
* **Obese:** BMI ≥ 30.0

### 2. BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)
BMR aapki body ki wo **base energy** hai jo bina koi physical kaam kiye, pure din bistar par lete rehne par bhi burn hoti hai (e.g., heart pumping, respiration, cellular repair).

### 3. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)
TDEE aapka **actual total daily calorie burn** hai. Isme aapka BMR + aapka daily physical movement (chalna, exercise, stairs chadhna, digest karna) shamil hota hai:

$$\text{TDEE} = \text{BMR} \times \text{Physical Activity Multiplier}$$

| Activity Level | Multiplier | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sedentary** | 1.2 | Desk job, minimal walking, koi exercise nahi |
| **Lightly Active** | 1.375 | 1–3 din light exercise ya walking |
| **Moderately Active** | 1.55 | 3–5 din moderate workout/gym |
| **Very Active** | 1.725 | 6–7 din intense sports/gym |
| **Extra Active** | 1.9 | Heavy physical labor ya athlete training |

## 2. Apna Ideal Weight Kaise Calculate Karein?

Agar aap 5 feet 9 inches (175 cm) ke hain, toh aapka target weight kya hona chahiye?

Iske do practical tareeqe hain:

### Method A: Healthy BMI Reverse Engineering
Agar hum standard healthy BMI range (21.5 – 23.0) lein:
$$\text{Ideal Weight (kg)} = \text{Target BMI} \times (\text{Height in meters})^2$$

175 cm (1.75 m) height ke vyakti ke liye:
$$\text{Target Weight} = 22 \times (1.75)^2 = 22 \times 3.0625 \approx 67.4\text{ kg}$$

Healthy range: **61 kg se 73 kg**.

### Method B: Hamwi & Devine Formula
Hamwi formula ke anusar:
* **Males:** 48 kg base (for 5 ft) + 2.7 kg per inch over 5 ft
* **Females:** 45.5 kg base (for 5 ft) + 2.2 kg per inch over 5 ft

## 3. Apne Fitness Goal ke Hisaab se Calories Kaise Set Karein?

Jab aapko apna TDEE pata chal jaye, toh aapka daily food intake aapke goal par nirbhar karta hai:

### Goal 1: Fat Loss / Weight Loss (Calorie Deficit)
Fat loss ka simple science hai: **Calories In < Calories Out**.
* **Moderate Deficit (Recommended):** TDEE − 300 se 500 kcal per day
* **Pace:** Lagbhag 0.4 kg se 0.5 kg fat loss per week
* **Rule:** Kabhi bhi apne **BMR** se kam calories na khayein. BMR se neeche jaane par muscle loss aur hormonal imbalance hone lagta hai.

### Goal 2: Muscle Gain / Bulking (Calorie Surplus)
Muscle mass build karne ke liye body ko extra fuel chahiye:
* **Lean Bulk:** TDEE + 200 se 300 kcal per day
* **Pace:** 0.25 kg – 0.5 kg per week (strength training ke saath)

### Goal 3: Weight Maintenance
* **Intake:** TDEE ke barabar calories khayein.

## 4. Step-by-Step Blueprint: Ek Practical Example

Maan lijiye Rahul ki profile ye hai:
- Age: 30 years | Gender: Male
- Height: 175 cm | Weight: 84 kg (BMI = 27.4, Overweight)
- Activity Level: Sedentary (Desk job)

### Step 1: BMR & TDEE Calculate Karein
- BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor): ~1,780 kcal
- TDEE (Sedentary × 1.2): **2,136 kcal / day**

### Step 2: Goal Target Set Karein (Weight Loss)
- Daily Calorie Target = $2,136 - 500 = \mathbf{1,636\text{ kcal / day}}$

### Step 3: Macronutrient Split (Protein, Carbs, Fats)
Sirf calories ginn-na kaafi nahi hai, nutrition ki quality bhi matter karti hai:
- **Protein (Muscle preservation):** 1.6g to 2.0g per kg ideal bodyweight (~120g – 135g) = ~500 kcal
- **Fats (Hormonal health):** 25% of total calories = ~45g = ~400 kcal
- **Carbohydrates (Daily energy):** Remaining calories = ~180g = ~720 kcal

## 5. Healthy Lifestyle Tips ke Saath Calculator ka Practical Use

1. **Food Logging & Measuring:** Katori ya chamach ke andaaze se calories aksar 30-40% galat hoti hain. Ek digital kitchen scale aur food tracking app use karein.
2. **NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis) Badhayein:** Sirf 45 minute gym kaafi nahi hai. Din bhar mein 8,000 se 10,000 steps chalne ka target rakhein.
3. **Protein Priority:** Har meal mein protein shamil karein (Paneer, eggs, chicken, soya, dal, greek yogurt). Protein digestion mein zyada energy kharch hoti hai (High TEF) aur pet lambe samay tak bhara rehta hai.
4. **Weekly Average par Dhyaan Dein:** Daily scale weight par paani ka asar hota hai. Har subah empty stomach weigh karein aur weekly average trend dekhein.
