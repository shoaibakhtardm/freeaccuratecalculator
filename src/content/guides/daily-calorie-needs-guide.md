## 1. The Two Halves of Energy Expenditure: BMR vs. TDEE

### 1. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)
Your BMR represents the baseline energy expenditure necessary to keep your cardiovascular system, lungs, brain, cellular turnover, and internal organs operating at rest in a thermoneutral environment. BMR accounts for **60% to 75%** of your total daily calories.

### 2. Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)
TDEE is the total number of calories you burn across a full 24-hour cycle. It consists of:
* **BMR:** 60–75%
* **NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis):** Fidgeting, walking, typing (15–20%)
* **TEF (Thermic Effect of Food):** Energy required to digest food (5–10%)
* **EAT (Exercise Activity Thermogenesis):** Formal workouts and training (5–15%)

$$\text{TDEE} = \text{BMR} \times \text{Physical Activity Multiplier}$$

---

## 2. The Clinical Formula: Mifflin-St Jeor Equation

The Mifflin-St Jeor formula is clinically validated as the most accurate estimation tool for healthy adults:

### For Men:
$$\text{BMR} = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age in years}) + 5$$

### For Women:
$$\text{BMR} = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age in years}) - 161$$

---

## 3. Physical Activity Multipliers

Once you compute your BMR, multiply by your true weekly physical activity factor:

| Activity Level | Multiplier | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sedentary** | $1.20$ | Desk job, little to no formal exercise |
| **Lightly Active** | $1.375$ | Light exercise or sports 1–3 days/week |
| **Moderately Active** | $1.55$ | Moderate exercise or training 3–5 days/week |
| **Very Active** | $1.725$ | Hard exercise 6–7 days/week |
| **Extremely Active** | $1.90$ | Heavy physical job plus 2x daily training |

## 4. Worked Calculation Example

Let's calculate for a 32-year-old male:
* **Weight:** 80 kg | **Height:** 180 cm | **Activity:** Moderately Active (3–5 gym sessions/week)

### Step 1: Calculate BMR
$$\text{BMR} = (10 \times 80) + (6.25 \times 180) - (5 \times 32) + 5$$
$$\text{BMR} = 800 + 1,125 - 160 + 5 = \mathbf{1,770\text{ kcal/day}}$$

### Step 2: Multiply by Activity Factor ($1.55$)
$$\text{TDEE} = 1,770 \times 1.55 = \mathbf{2,743.5\text{ calories/day}}$$

This individual can consume **~2,740 calories daily** without gaining or losing weight.

## 5. Weight Loss and Muscle Gain Mathematics

* **Fat Loss Deficit (1 lb / week):** $2,740 - 500 = \mathbf{2,240\text{ calories/day}}$
* **Aggressive Fat Loss (1.5 lbs / week):** $2,740 - 750 = \mathbf{1,990\text{ calories/day}}$
* **Lean Muscle Bulk:** $2,740 + 300 = \mathbf{3,040\text{ calories/day}}$
